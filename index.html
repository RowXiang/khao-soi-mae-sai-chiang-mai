A SMALL PLACE WITH A BIG BOWL

Come for the curry. Stay for the rhythm of the street.

Mae Sai sits in a small lane in Chiang Mai, where the best plan is often simple: find a table, order a bowl, add pickles and let the city carry on around you.

The shop is best suited to casual dine-in, a quick meal, or takeaway. No polished ceremony — just a northern Thai staple made for everyday eating.
